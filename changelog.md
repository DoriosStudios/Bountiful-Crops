# Bountiful Crops v1.1.0

This update rebalances Bountiful Crops, adds area-based crop acceleration, and introduces native compatibility with Bountiful Bonsais.

## HIGHLIGHTS
- Rebalanced all resource crops and their four progression tiers.
- Added native compatibility with Bountiful Bonsais.
- Added the Pedestal and three tiers of Accelerator Clocks.
- Improved crop harvesting, growth, and seed handling.

---

## GAMEPLAY
- Mature crops can be harvested without replanting and support Fortune.
- Breaking a mature crop returns the seed required to replant it.
- Bountiful Crops seeds now work in Bountiful Bonsais with matching visuals and drops.
- Gold, Diamond, and Nether Star Accelerator Clocks provide increasingly stronger growth bonuses in a 9x9 area.
- Pedestals accelerate Bountiful Crops, vanilla crops, and compatible custom crops.
