# Bountiful Crops v1.1.0

This update brings a general rebalance to Bountiful Crops, introduces Pedestals and Accelerator Clocks for area-based crop growth, and adds native compatibility with Bountiful Bonsais.

## HIGHLIGHTS
- Rebalanced resource crops and their progression.
- Added native compatibility with Bountiful Bonsais.
- Added the Pedestal and three tiers of Accelerator Clocks.
- Improved crop harvesting, growth, and seed handling.

---

## CROPS
- Rebalanced resource crops across their four progression tiers.
- Adjusted growth and production balance to better reflect each crop tier.
- Higher-tier crops now receive appropriately scaled growth acceleration.
- Mature crops can be harvested directly and automatically return to their first growth stage.
- Mature crops return the seed needed to replant them when broken.
- Added Fortune support to resource crop harvesting.

## BONSAI COMPATIBILITY
- Added native compatibility with Bountiful Bonsais.
- Bountiful Crops seeds can now be used in supported Bonsais.
- Resource Crop Bonsais use their matching crop visuals and resource drops.
- Bonsai growth speed and output respect the soil and plant balance of both addons.

## PEDESTALS AND ACCELERATOR CLOCKS
- Added the Pedestal, a new block used to accelerate crops in a surrounding area.
- Added three Accelerator Clock tiers:
  - Gold Accelerator Clock.
  - Diamond Accelerator Clock.
  - Nether Star Accelerator Clock.
- Higher-tier Accelerator Clocks provide stronger crop growth acceleration.
- Pedestals affect crops in a 9x9 area centered on the block.
- Placing an Accelerator Clock on a Pedestal briefly displays its affected area.
- Accelerator Clocks can be safely removed from their Pedestal and are returned when the Pedestal is broken.
- Added support for accelerating Bountiful Crops, vanilla crops, and compatible custom crops.

## GENERAL
- Updated crop drops, seeds, soils, recipes, and progression.
- Improved crop growth particles and visual feedback.
- Improved the safety of Pedestal and Accelerator Clock interactions.
- Fixed inconsistent crop growth and harvesting behavior.
